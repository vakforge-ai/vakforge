# Code of conduct

vakforge adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, and any other project space, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give feedback on the work rather than the person, and do not harass anyone.

Two points specific to this project:

- Voice data is personal data. Never post real customer recordings, transcripts, names or IDs in issues or pull requests.
- Locale packs are written by people from many markets. Criticise the rules, not the culture they come from.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to **vakforge.ai@gmail.com** with the subject line `CONDUCT: <short summary>`. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will respond, investigate, and act as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.

# Sharma Electronics: customer FAQ

Sharma Electronics sells inverters, batteries, fans and small appliances from two shops in Jaipur and delivers across Rajasthan.

## Delivery kitne din mein hoti hai?

Jaipur city mein same day ya agle din. Baaki Rajasthan mein 2 se 4 din. Order confirm hone ke baad aapko WhatsApp par tracking link milega.

## Refund aur return

Product agar damaged ya galat aaya hai toh 7 din ke andar return kar sakte hain. Refund usi payment method mein 5 se 7 working days mein aata hai. Inverter aur battery install hone ke baad return nahi hote, sirf warranty claim hota hai.

## Warranty

Inverter par 2 saal, battery par 3 saal (brand ke hisaab se), fan par 1 saal. Warranty claim ke liye invoice number aur serial number chahiye. Bill ke bina claim nahi hoga.

## Payment

Cash on delivery ₹15,000 tak. Usse upar UPI, card ya bank transfer. EMI 3, 6 aur 9 mahine ke liye ₹10,000 se upar ke order par available hai.

## Installation

Inverter aur battery ki installation free hai Jaipur mein. Bahar ke liye ₹500 charge lagta hai. Technician delivery ke 24 ghante ke andar aata hai.
